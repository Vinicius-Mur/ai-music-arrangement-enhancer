## 🧠 Methodology: Prompt-Driven Audio Architecture

Generative audio models rely heavily on how the text prompt is structured. By utilizing a hybrid approach of **lyrical input** and **bracketed audio metatags**, I guide the AI's arrangement, instrumentation, pacing, and vocal delivery. 

The methodology involves:
1.  **Structural Tagging:** Defining clear boundaries for verses, choruses, and bridges.
2.  **Instrumentation Cues:** Prompting specific instruments and textures (e.g., *Tribal Synth Lead*, *Toy Piano*).
3.  **Vocal Directing:** Instructing the model on the emotional delivery and persona (e.g., *Deep Clear Vocal* vs. *Innocent Childlike Vocal*).
4.  **Dynamic Shifts:** Forcing the AI to build tension or drop the beat exactly where the narrative demands it.
